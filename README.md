## 📊 Project Overview

This Excel-based project analyzes bakery sales performance using an interactive dashboard. It provides insights into revenue, product performance, customer behavior, and sales trends to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales performance  
- Identify top and low-performing products  
- Understand customer purchasing patterns  
- Evaluate payment method distribution  
- Track weekly and daily sales trends  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Interactive product filtering (Bread, Cake, Cookies)  
- Revenue breakdown by product  
- Quantity analysis per product  
- Payment method comparison (Card, Cash, Transfer)  
- Customer segmentation (New vs Returning)  
- Weekly and daily sales trend analysis  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- 🥇 Cake is the highest revenue-generating product  
- 🍪 Cookies contribute significantly to total sales  
- 🍞 Bread has the lowest revenue performance  
- 🔁 Returning customers drive majority of sales (76%)  
- 💳 Card payments are the most used method  
- 📉 Sales peak at Week 3 before declining  
- 📅 Monday records the highest daily revenue  

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Products
Prioritize marketing for **Cake**, the top revenue driver.

### 2. Improve Bread Sales
Introduce combo deals and discounts to increase demand.

### 3. Strengthen Customer Loyalty
Implement reward programs to retain returning customers.

### 4. Optimize Inventory Management
Stock high-performing products adequately and reduce wastage.

### 5. Expand Digital Payment Options
Improve card and mobile payment integration for convenience.

### 6. Investigate Sales Drop
Analyze causes of decline after Week 3.
